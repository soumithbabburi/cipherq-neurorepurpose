# RepurposeIQ — Demo Video Scripts

For the BIO International Convention demo. Three assets:
1. Full narration (~2:45) — main demo voiceover
2. 60-second teaser — social / intro cut
3. On-screen caption track — text overlays synced to the voiceover

**Voiceover / TTS notes**
- Pace ~155 words/min; set TTS rate slightly below default; pause at each `[SCENE]` break.
- Pronunciation: "ALCOA-plus" (not spelled out), "GAMP-5" → "gamp five",
  "505(b)(2)" → write for the TTS engine as **"five-oh-five-b-two"**.
- Claims validated *data* and explainable *evidence* — never that predictions are
  clinically proven. Keep that line; it's what keeps a technical audience trusting.
- Sources: "knowledge graph" stays generic; ChEMBL implied as "curated
  pharmacology"; IUPHAR named only because the 91% concordance needs a named referee.

## 1. Full narration (≈ 2 min 45 s, ~430 words)

**[SCENE: Title / logo]**
Every year, the pharmaceutical industry spends billions discovering new drugs —
while thousands of approved, safe compounds sit unused against diseases they could
treat. That's the promise of drug repurposing. The bottleneck has never been ideas.
It's trust — trust in the data behind those ideas. This is RepurposeIQ.

**[SCENE: Home — research areas grid]**
RepurposeIQ turns a disease into a ranked list of repurposing candidates, in
seconds. You simply choose a therapeutic area. Behind that one click, the platform
brings together decades of curated pharmacology, biological networks, and clinical
evidence — and scores every drug against the disease.

**[SCENE: Candidate results / score cards]**
For each candidate, RepurposeIQ weighs four independent signals. Does the drug
already have clinical evidence for this disease? Does it hit the right biological
targets? How potent is it? And — most importantly — does the biology connect it to
the disease, through a knowledge graph of more than two million drug, gene, and
disease relationships?

**[SCENE: A "novel" candidate card with the gene badge]**
And it doesn't just confirm the obvious. RepurposeIQ surfaces *novel* candidates —
drugs never before indicated for the disease, but linked to it through shared
disease genes. And it shows you the exact genes that make the connection. Every
suggestion is explainable. Never a black box.

**[SCENE: Candidate dossier / analysis — docking, PBPK]**
Open any candidate, and you get a full evidence dossier. Molecular docking against
the target's real structure. Physiologically-based pharmacokinetic modeling. A
probability-of-success assessment grounded in the drug's development stage. The
science — transparent, and in one place.

**[SCENE: Data Validation page]**
But here is what sets RepurposeIQ apart. Pharma teams don't trust public data — and
they're right to be cautious. So we don't ask them to trust it. We measure it. Every
dataset is validated to the same standards regulated pharma applies to its own
systems — ALCOA-plus, and GAMP-5 — and the proof is published live, inside the
platform.

**[SCENE: the 91% concordance panel]**
Our drug-potency data agrees with an independent expert database, IUPHAR, ninety-one
percent of the time. Its measurement noise sits within the known experimental limit
of the field itself. And disease-term matching is one hundred percent correct
against a curated ground-truth set. Every number is re-runnable. Every finding —
including our weaknesses — is openly disclosed.

**[SCENE: Suite view / CompoundIQ]**
And RepurposeIQ is only half the story. Its companion, CompoundIQ, screens compounds
for 505(b)(2) reformulation opportunities — built on the very same validated data
foundation. One trusted data layer. Two purpose-built engines.

**[SCENE: Close / logo + tagline]**
Repurposing's barrier was never imagination. It was evidence you could trust.
RepurposeIQ delivers both. Let's put your next breakthrough back to work.

## 4. Full platform walkthrough — every feature & evidence base (≈ 5 min, ~800 words)

The complete demo. Covers all eight evidence bases and every core feature, in
workflow order. Naming: standard public sources are named (as on the in-platform
Documentation page); the proprietary knowledge graph stays generic.

**[SCENE: Landing → "Launch Platform"]**
Bringing a new drug to market takes more than a decade and billions of dollars. Yet
thousands of already-approved, safety-tested molecules may work against diseases
they were never designed for. RepurposeIQ finds them — and backs every suggestion
with transparent, validated evidence. Let's walk through it.

**[SCENE: Dashboard / research areas]**
RepurposeIQ works in two directions: start from a *disease* to find candidate drugs,
or start from a *molecule* to find new indications. Both converge on a single
evidence dossier. Let's begin with a disease.

**[SCENE: Select a disease → ranked candidates]**
Choose a therapeutic area, and within seconds the platform ranks repurposing
candidates. Each one earns a relevance score from zero to one, combining four
independent signals. Indication evidence — has the drug already reached a clinical
phase for this disease. Target relevance — do its protein targets overlap the
disease's biology. Activity strength — how potently it binds, from measured potency
data. And network evidence — does a biomedical knowledge graph connect the drug to
the disease through shared genes and pathways.

**[SCENE: Data Explorer / "data footprint"]**
Those signals rest on a deep evidence base. Curated bioactivity, mechanism, and
clinical-phase data from ChEMBL. Disease-gene associations and pathways from Open
Targets. Clinical trials from ClinicalTrials-dot-gov. Literature from PubMed.
Disease vocabulary from MeSH. Protein structures from the Protein Data Bank. The FDA
Orange Book for patents and exclusivity. And post-market safety signals from openFDA.
Eight authoritative sources — every value traceable to its origin.

**[SCENE: A "novel" candidate card showing the connecting genes]**
And RepurposeIQ doesn't just rank the obvious. It *discovers* novel candidates —
drugs never indicated for the disease, but linked to it through shared disease genes
in the knowledge graph — and it shows you the exact genes that make the connection.
Discovery, not just confirmation.

**[SCENE: Open a candidate → evidence dossier]**
Open any candidate, and you get its evidence dossier — the single integrated artifact
for that drug-disease pair. The score and its breakdown, the mechanistic rationale,
the physicochemical and developability profile, literature, trials, post-market
safety, the regulatory pathway, and the IP landscape — consolidated in one place.

**[SCENE: In-silico tab — docking]**
From the dossier, you can validate in silico. Where a protein structure exists, the
candidate is docked into the named binding pocket and scored with a Vina-style
free-energy estimate. And if no suitable structure exists, the platform says so —
rather than fabricating a number.

**[SCENE: PBPK simulation]**
A physiologically-based pharmacokinetic model simulates how the drug distributes
through the body over time, and estimates free-drug occupancy at the target against
its measured binding affinity.

**[SCENE: Quantum properties]**
And electronic descriptors are computed with a real semi-empirical quantum-chemistry
method. Docking, exposure, and electronic plausibility — three independent lines of
in-silico confirmation.

**[SCENE: Knowledge graph page]**
The knowledge graph makes the entire rationale explicit — tracing the chain from the
drug, through its targets and pathways, to the disease. Every link is visible.
Nothing is a black box.

**[SCENE: Regulatory verdict]**
For the business case, RepurposeIQ maps the regulatory path. A single reconciled
verdict separates whether the *molecule* is approved — so its safety package can be
cited — from whether it's approved *for this indication* — the novelty question. And
it lays out the pathway per region: the five-oh-five-b-two route in the United
States, the hybrid application in Europe, and the approved-abroad route in India.

**[SCENE: Acquisition signal]**
The acquisition signal, grounded in FDA Orange Book facts, tells you whether the
molecule's rights can realistically be obtained — patents on file, generic filings,
and whether the asset has been shelved and is ripe for in-licensing.

**[SCENE: Signature connectivity]**
Signature connectivity asks a deeper question — does the drug's biological effect
*reverse* the disease's, the core idea behind the Connectivity Map. A strong reversal
flags a mechanistically-grounded candidate.

**[SCENE: Probability of success]**
And every candidate carries a probability of clinical success — a phase-transition
model built on published industry success rates, crediting repurposing candidates
for the safety they've already cleared.

**[SCENE: Data Validation page]**
But the feature that matters most to a pharma partner is this. We don't ask you to
trust the data — we measure it. Every dataset is validated to the same standards
regulated pharma uses on its own systems — ALCOA-plus and GAMP-5. Our potency data
agrees with an independent expert database, IUPHAR, ninety-one percent of the time.
Disease-term matching is one hundred percent correct against a curated ground-truth
set. And it's published live, re-runnable, with every finding openly disclosed.

**[SCENE: CompoundIQ]**
RepurposeIQ shares this validated data foundation with a companion platform,
CompoundIQ, for five-oh-five-b-two reformulation screening. One trusted data layer;
two purpose-built engines.

**[SCENE: Close / logo]**
From a disease to a validated, fundable repurposing hypothesis — with the evidence to
defend every step. That's RepurposeIQ. Let's put your next breakthrough back to work.
