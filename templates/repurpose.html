Repurpose a Molecule

Enter a molecule you already hold approval for. We derive its protein targets, then surface new candidate indications ranked by mechanistic and clinical evidence — the 505(b)(2) reverse search.

Candidates are drawn from three sources — genetic associations (Open Targets), existing clinical trials of this drug (ClinicalTrials.gov), and literature co-mention (PubMed) — and ranked by combined mechanistic + clinical evidence. The drug's global approval status informs 505(b)(2) feasibility (in the dossier), not the ranking.
