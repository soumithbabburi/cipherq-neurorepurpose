Repurpose a Molecule

Enter a molecule you already hold approval for. We derive its protein targets, then surface new candidate indications ranked by mechanistic and clinical evidence, the 505(b)(2) reverse search.

Candidates are drawn from four sources: genetic associations (a target association resource), existing clinical trials of this drug (ClinicalTrials.gov), literature co mention (PubMed), and knowledge graphs (Hetionet, DRKG, and PrimeKG's 17,080 diseases), then ranked by combined mechanistic + clinical evidence. The drug's global approval status informs 505(b)(2) feasibility (in the dossier), not the ranking.
