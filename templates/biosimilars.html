Which biologics are worth copying

Every FDA-licensed reference biologic, ranked as a biosimilar development target. We combine the reference-product exclusivity cliff (when competition can enter), the whitespace (how few biosimilars have already launched), and the franchise size into an opportunity score. A high score with an open cliff and zero entrants is open whitespace.

Source: FDA Purple Book (licensed biological products, biosimilar/interchangeable status, reference-product exclusivity). Cliff = explicit reference-product exclusivity date where FDA provides one, otherwise the 12-year BPCIA exclusivity from first licensure. Interchangeable status from the FDA interchangeable-approval field.
